# Hardship grants and emergency support

If money is tight and it's getting in the way of your study — rent, food, power, an unexpected bill — there's help available. You don't need to be at rock bottom to ask.

## When to use this

Apply if:

- You can't cover rent, food, or essential bills this week or month
- An unexpected cost has come up (medical, dental, car repair, family emergency)
- Your work hours have dropped and you can't make ends meet
- You've had to travel home suddenly for a tangihanga or family crisis

This isn't a loan. Most hardship support is a one-off grant you don't pay back.

## Your options

### Aotearoa University Hardship Grant
A one-off grant for enrolled students facing short-term financial pressure. Apply through **My Support → Financial Assistance** in the Student Portal. You'll be asked about your situation, current income, and what the money is for. Most applications get a decision within 5 working days.

### Emergency food and essentials
If you need food or essentials this week, the Wellbeing team can connect you with the campus food support service the same day. Message **Otto** or drop in to the Wellbeing hub — no appointment needed, no questions asked.

### Studylink — government student support
If you're a domestic student and you haven't already, check what you're eligible for through **Studylink** (the Ministry of Social Development's student finance service). They administer Student Allowance, Student Loan living costs, and hardship payments for students. Visit studylink.govt.nz or call them directly — the campus team can help you work out what to ask for.

### Talk to a financial mentor
Free, confidential one-on-one sessions with someone who can help you map out your money, talk to creditors on your behalf, or set up a plan. Book through the Wellbeing hub.

## What happens next

When you apply for a hardship grant, you'll get a confirmation email and a follow-up from someone on the financial wellbeing team. They may ask a few questions to understand the situation — this isn't a test, it's so they can match you to the right kind of help. Approved grants are usually paid into your bank account within 3 working days.
