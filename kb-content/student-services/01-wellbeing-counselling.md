# Talk to someone now — wellbeing and counselling at Aotearoa University

If things are heavy right now — stress, low mood, anxiety, something happening at home, or you just need to talk — you don't have to figure it out alone. Here's how to reach a real person.

## When to use this

Use this article if you're struggling and want support. You don't need to be in crisis to ask for help. Feeling flat, overwhelmed by deadlines, lonely, or not sleeping well are all good reasons to reach out.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call **111** now.

## Your options

### Talk to a university counsellor
Free, confidential, and available to all enrolled students. Counsellors can help with anything from exam stress to grief, relationship stuff, or longer-term mental health support. Book through the Student Wellbeing portal or by contacting the Wellbeing team directly. Most students get a first appointment within a week.

### Message Otto, our student assistant
If you want to test the water before talking to a human, you can message **Otto** any time of day. Otto can listen, suggest next steps, and connect you to a counsellor or after-hours service if that's what you need. Otto won't share what you say with academic staff.

### Call or text 1737 — free, any time
**1737** is Aotearoa's national mental health and addictions helpline. Trained counsellors answer 24/7. It's free from any phone, including mobiles with no credit. Text the same number if calling feels like too much.

### After-hours and urgent help
Outside of weekday hours, contact **Healthline** on **0800 611 116** for free health advice, or your nearest hospital emergency department for urgent mental health concerns.

## What happens next

When you book with a counsellor, you'll get a short intake form to fill in — this just helps the counsellor understand what you'd like to talk about. The first session is usually 50 minutes. Everything you share stays between you and the counsellor unless there's a serious safety concern. You decide whether to come back.
