# Install Microsoft 365 on your personal devices

As an enrolled student you get Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Outlook, Teams) free for the duration of your studies. You can install it on up to 5 PCs or Macs, 5 tablets and 5 phones using your student account. This article walks you through installing the desktop apps on a laptop.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your student email and password
- MFA enrolled on your phone (you'll be prompted to verify)
- About 4 GB of free disk space and a stable internet connection
- Admin rights on the device (your own laptop is fine — a flatmate's locked-down work machine isn't)

## Steps

1. Open a browser and go to `office.com`.
2. Click **Sign in** in the top right.
3. Enter your full student email address (e.g. `firstname.lastname@student.[Placeholder: your university domain]`). You'll be redirected to the university sign-in page — enter your password and approve the MFA prompt.
   [Screenshot: Microsoft 365 home page after sign-in, showing app tiles and Install button]
4. Click **Install apps** (top right) → **Microsoft 365 apps**.
5. Run the installer that downloads (`OfficeSetup.exe` on Windows or `.pkg` on Mac). Follow the prompts — it'll take 5-15 minutes depending on your connection.
6. Once installed, open any app (Word is fine) and sign in with your student email when prompted. This activates your licence.
7. To get Outlook, Teams or OneNote on your phone, install each from the App Store / Play Store and sign in with the same student email.

## Still stuck?

- **"You don't have a licence" error** — sign out of Office completely (File → Account → Sign out in any Office app), close all Office apps, then sign back in. If that fails, you may have hit the 5-device limit — go to `office.com` → **My account** → **Devices** and sign out an old device.
- **Already had a personal Office account on this device?** Uninstall the personal version first, then install the student one. Running both side-by-side causes activation loops.
- **Slow download** — university wifi is faster than home. Install while on campus if you can.
- **Graduating soon?** Your licence ends a few months after your final enrolment. Save anything important to OneDrive or a personal account before then.

For licensing or activation issues, contact the IT helpdesk via the student portal.
