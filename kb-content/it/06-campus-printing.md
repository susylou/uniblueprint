# Print from a personal laptop or phone (campus printing)

You can send a print job from your own laptop or phone and pick it up at any campus printer using your student ID card. This article covers the most common setup: web upload (works on any device, no install needed). It's the quickest way to get something printed when you're on the way to a tutorial.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your student email and password
- Print credit on your account — check at `[Placeholder: campus print portal URL, e.g. print.aotearoa.ac.nz]`. Top up via the student portal or at any kiosk.
- Your student ID card (for releasing the job at the printer)
- The file you want to print, as a PDF if possible (best compatibility)

## Steps

### Send the job from your device

1. Go to `[Placeholder: campus print portal URL]` and sign in with your student email.
2. Click **Web Print** → **Submit a Job**.
   [Screenshot: print portal Web Print page with Submit button]
3. Choose your settings:
   - **Printer queue**: `Student-BW` (black & white) or `Student-Colour` (colour, costs more)
   - **Copies, double-sided, paper size** — adjust as needed
4. Click **Upload Documents** and drag your file in (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, JPG and PNG all work).
5. Click **Upload & Complete**. You'll see "Held in a queue" — that's correct. Jobs are held for 24 hours.

### Release the job at the printer

1. Find any campus printer (libraries, labs, most student spaces).
2. Tap your student ID card on the card reader. The printer screen logs you in.
   [Screenshot: printer touch screen showing "Print Release" with job list]
3. Select your job from the list and tap **Print**. The cost is deducted from your credit.
4. Don't forget your card — most printers will beep at you, but not all.

## Still stuck?

- **Job not showing at the printer?** Wait 30 seconds and refresh. If still missing, re-submit via the portal — uploads occasionally fail silently.
- **Out of credit?** Top up in the student portal. There's no overdraft.
- **File won't upload?** PDF is most reliable. If you're printing from Pages or Google Docs, export to PDF first.
- **Card not recognised?** New cards take 24 hours to register with the print system. Use the **Username + password** option on the printer screen as a fallback.
- **Need to scan instead?** Tap your card → **Scan to Email** → confirm your address.

For ongoing issues, log a ticket via the student portal — include the printer location.
