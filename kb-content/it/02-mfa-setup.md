# Set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) on your phone

MFA adds a second check when you sign in — usually a tap on your phone — so even if someone gets your password, they can't get into your account. The university requires MFA for student email, the LMS, and Office 365. This walks you through enrolling with the Microsoft Authenticator app, which is the recommended option.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your smartphone (iOS or Android)
- Your student username and current password
- A second device or browser tab (laptop, library PC) to scan the QR code — easier than doing it all on one screen

If you don't have a smartphone, see **Still stuck?** for the SMS fallback.

## Steps

1. On your phone, install **Microsoft Authenticator** from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). Don't open it yet.
2. On your laptop, go to `[Placeholder: your university MFA enrolment URL, e.g. aka.ms/mfasetup]` and sign in with your student email.
3. Choose **Add sign-in method** → **Authenticator app** → **Next**.
   [Screenshot: security info page with Add sign-in method button]
4. Open Microsoft Authenticator on your phone. Tap the **+** in the top right, then **Work or school account**, then **Scan QR code**. Allow camera access if prompted.
5. Point your phone camera at the QR code on your laptop screen.
   [Screenshot: QR code shown on laptop with Authenticator scan view on phone]
6. The website will send a test notification to your phone — tap **Approve**, then enter the number shown on screen if asked.
7. Back on your laptop, add a **phone number** as a backup method (in case you lose your phone). Use a number you actually answer.
8. Sign out and back in to your student email to test. You should get a prompt on your phone within a few seconds.

## Still stuck?

- **No notification?** Check your phone is on wifi or mobile data, and that Authenticator has notification permissions in your phone settings.
- **Got a new phone?** Re-enrol before you wipe the old one. If you've already lost access, you'll need to verify in person at the IT helpdesk with your student ID.
- **No smartphone?** Choose **Phone (SMS)** as your method instead — you'll get a code by text. Less secure but supported.

For anything else, contact the IT helpdesk via the student portal.
