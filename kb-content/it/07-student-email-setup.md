# Set up your student email on phone and forwarding

Your student email is the official channel for everything from assignment notifications to exam timetables — so you need to actually see it. This article covers adding your student email to your phone using Outlook (the recommended app), plus how to forward to a personal address if you'd rather read it there.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your full student email address (e.g. `firstname.lastname@student.[Placeholder: your university domain]`)
- Your student password
- MFA enrolled on your phone

Note: the university requires you to **read** your student email regularly. Forwarding is allowed, but you're still responsible for messages sent to the student address.

## Steps

### Add student email to your phone (Outlook app)

1. Install **Microsoft Outlook** from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
2. Open the app. Tap **Add Account** (or **Get Started** if it's your first time).
3. Enter your full student email and tap **Continue**.
4. When redirected to the university sign-in page, enter your password and approve the MFA prompt.
   [Screenshot: Outlook mobile app showing university sign-in screen with MFA prompt]
5. Skip "Add another account" if asked. Allow notifications — you want these.
6. Your inbox should load within a few seconds. Calendar and contacts sync automatically.

### Apple Mail (iOS) — alternative

If you'd rather use the built-in Mail app:

1. **Settings** → **Mail** → **Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Microsoft Exchange**.
2. Enter your student email and a description (e.g. "Uni").
3. Tap **Sign In** (not Configure Manually). Approve MFA when prompted.
4. Choose what to sync (Mail, Calendar, Contacts) and tap **Save**.

### Set up forwarding to a personal email

1. In a browser, go to `outlook.office.com` and sign in with your student account.
2. Click the **gear icon** (top right) → **View all Outlook settings**.
3. Go to **Mail** → **Forwarding**.
4. Tick **Enable forwarding**, enter your personal email, and tick **Keep a copy of forwarded messages**.
   [Screenshot: Outlook web forwarding settings page with options ticked]
5. Click **Save**. Send yourself a test message to confirm it arrives.

## Still stuck?

- **"Account not found" on Outlook mobile** — double-check you're using the full `@student.[domain]` address, not just your username.
- **Emails not arriving on personal address?** Check spam first. Some providers (especially Gmail) classify university forwards as promotional.
- **Forwarding option missing?** Some faculties (e.g. Health Sciences) disable forwarding for privacy reasons. You'll need to read email in Outlook.
- **MFA loop?** Sign out of the Outlook app and back in. If it keeps prompting, reset your MFA method.

For anything else, contact the IT helpdesk via the student portal.
