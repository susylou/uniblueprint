# Access your LMS — login issues, missing courses, enrolments

Your LMS (`[Placeholder: your university's LMS, e.g. Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard]`) is where you'll find course content, submit assignments and check grades. This article covers the three most common issues: you can't log in, a course is missing, or you've just enrolled and don't see anything yet.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your student username and password
- Your enrolment confirmation email (the one from the Student Centre when you signed up for papers)

## Steps

### Can't log in

1. Go to `[Placeholder: your LMS URL, e.g. learn.aotearoa.ac.nz]`.
2. Click **Sign in with student account** — don't use a personal email or Google account, even if those options show.
   [Screenshot: LMS login page highlighting the student SSO button]
3. Enter your student email and password, then approve the MFA prompt on your phone.
4. If you see "account not provisioned", your LMS profile hasn't been created yet — see the next section.

### A course is missing

Courses appear in your LMS 24-48 hours after you enrol. If it's been longer:

1. Sign in to the **Student Centre** at `[Placeholder: student admin URL]`.
2. Check **My Enrolments** — is the course actually listed?
   - **Yes**: the course may be hidden by the teacher until the trimester start date. Check the start date on your enrolment record.
   - **No**: you're not enrolled. Re-enrol via Student Centre, or contact your faculty's student advisor.
3. After enrolment, give it up to 24 hours, then sign out of the LMS and sign back in.

### Just enrolled — nothing showing

This is normal. The LMS syncs overnight. If you enrolled today, your courses should appear tomorrow morning. Late additions to a course (after the trimester has started) sync within a few hours.

## Still stuck?

- **"Course locked" or "Not yet available"** — the teacher hasn't released it. Wait until the official start date or email them directly.
- **You can see the course but can't open content** — try a different browser (Chrome and Edge work best). Clear your cache.
- **Fees on hold?** Outstanding fees block LMS access in some faculties. Check the Student Centre for any alerts.
- **Mobile app issues** — the LMS website in a browser is more reliable than the app for troubleshooting.

If your course is still missing after 48 hours and your enrolment is confirmed, log a ticket via the student portal and include your student ID and the course code.
