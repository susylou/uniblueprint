# Reset your student password or unlock your account

Forgotten your password, or locked out after too many wrong tries? You can sort both of these yourself in a couple of minutes — no need to call IT.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your student ID (the number on your student card, e.g. `12345678`)
- Access to either your registered mobile number **or** your personal recovery email
- A device with an internet connection (your phone is fine)

If you've never set a recovery method, skip to **Still stuck?** below.

## Steps

1. Go to the self-service password portal at `[Placeholder: your university password reset URL, e.g. password.aotearoa.ac.nz]`.
2. Choose **Forgot password** or **Unlock account** — they share the same flow.
3. Enter your student username (usually `firstname.lastname` or your student ID) and complete the CAPTCHA.
   [Screenshot: password portal landing page with username field and the two action buttons]
4. Pick a verification method — **text to mobile** or **email to recovery address**. A 6-digit code arrives within a minute.
5. Enter the code, then set a new password. It must be at least 12 characters and include a mix of upper case, lower case, numbers and a symbol. Don't reuse your last 5 passwords.
   [Screenshot: new password field with strength indicator]
6. Sign back in to a university service (LMS, email, or the student portal) to confirm it works.

Account unlocks are automatic once you complete the reset — you don't need a separate step.

## Still stuck?

- **No verification methods set up?** You'll need to verify in person. Bring your student ID to the IT helpdesk at `[Placeholder: campus IT location]` or use the live chat on the student portal during business hours.
- **Code didn't arrive?** Check spam, confirm your mobile number is current in the student portal, and try the other method.
- **Password keeps being rejected?** Avoid your name, student ID, or anything from your last 5 passwords.

If none of the above works, log a ticket via the student portal or chat to IT.
