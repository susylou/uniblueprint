Design language
Aotearoa University · Pacific-coastal palette · serif display + sans body · warm, plain, bilingual

Warm, coastal, considered. Brand colour as accent, not flood. Cream paper backdrop instead of cold white so the portal reads as a calm place, not a service desk.

Voice & bilingual treatment

  • Plain English — "Talk to Otto", not "Initiate conversation with intake assistant".
  • Bilingual lock-ups — English + Te Reo treated as a typographic pair, not a toggle.
  • No system jargon — "Things you're working on", not "My cases". Case ID stays available but de-emphasised.
  • One agent, many registers. Otto is the single assistant across the portal — tone adapts to context. In Wellbeing mode: "That sounds really hard." In IT mode: "Got it — password reset, check your phone." Same Otto, same shell, different pace.
  • Te Reo is design-placeholder — flagged for cultural review before real audience use.

Reading the difference. One agent — Otto — across the whole portal. Same student, same shell, same brand. In Wellbeing mode, Otto opens with a feeling-check and offers space; in IT mode, Otto opens with a problem-check and acts. The warm gradient (teal → terracotta) and the cool gradient (teal → pounamu) are the only consistent visual signal that the register has shifted. The student doesn't learn two interfaces — they get the right tone for the moment from the same trusted assistant, without losing context.
